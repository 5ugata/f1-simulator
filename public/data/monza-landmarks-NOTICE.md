`monza-osm-paddock.json` is the unchanged response downloaded on 3 October 2026 from the [public OpenStreetMap map API](https://api.openstreetmap.org/api/0.6/map.json?bbox=9.279,45.613,9.286,45.622). Its geographic bounding box is 9.279°–9.286° E, 45.613°–45.622° N. Objects crossing the box may extend outside it. Each original element retains its source ID, version, timestamp and tags.

`monza-landmarks.json` is a derived database under the same ODbL 1.0 license. The 16 closed building footprints copy the source ways' node coordinates exactly; the two tower points copy their source-node coordinates exactly. The selected object IDs and their individual source URLs are supplied with every feature. All files are served locally under `/data/` and may be downloaded and reused under these terms.

Heights, facade materials, openings, staircases and the tower footprint dimensions are authored additions, explicitly identified in the derived data. No Google Earth or Street View imagery or geometry is redistributed in these files. The separate circuit centerline in `monza.geojson` retains its MIT license; these map references do not replace it.
