# OpenStreetMap bridge reference

© OpenStreetMap contributors. Data is available under the [Open Database License 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/). See [OpenStreetMap copyright and license](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright).

The following files were downloaded unchanged from the OpenStreetMap API on 29 September 2026:

- `monza-osm-way-34404729.json`: [historic elevated raceway segment](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/34404729), version 9; its two nodes have version 3.
- `monza-osm-way-1443867793.json`: [covered Grand Prix raceway segment](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/1443867793), version 1; its two nodes have version 1.

`monza-banking-bridge.json` is the adapted bridge-reference data derived from these two segments and is also made available under ODbL 1.0. It contains the calculated crossing coordinate, bearing and derivation. All three JSON files are served locally under `/data/` and may be downloaded and reused under those terms.

Only bridge placement and orientation use these OpenStreetMap extracts. The separate `monza.geojson` road dataset retains its documented MIT provenance. The bridge's procedural mesh, dimensions and height are authored approximations; the source map is not a survey and does not provide those measurements.
